# Northwind Labs IT Security Policy

This policy keeps company and customer data safe. It applies to every employee, contractor and device that connects to company systems.

## Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across services. Use the company password manager to generate and store them. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, source control, the VPN and every system that holds customer data. Never share your password or MFA codes, including with IT staff.

## Devices

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption, automatic updates and the endpoint protection agent enabled. Lock your screen whenever you step away; screens lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may access email and chat only through the managed mobile app.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer data is always Restricted. Restricted data must not be stored on personal devices, shared through personal email, or pasted into external AI tools that are not approved by the security team.

## Approved AI tools

Only AI tools on the approved list may be used with Internal or Confidential data. The list is maintained by the security team on the intranet. Requests to add a tool go through the security review form and take about 2 weeks.

## Reporting incidents

Report a suspected security incident, a lost device or a phishing email to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of noticing it. Do not try to investigate on your own. Lost or stolen devices are wiped remotely.

## Access reviews

Managers review their team's access to systems every quarter. Access is removed within 24 hours when someone leaves the company.
