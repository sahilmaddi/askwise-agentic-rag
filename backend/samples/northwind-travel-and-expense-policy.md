# Northwind Labs Travel and Expense Policy

This policy explains what Northwind Labs reimburses when employees travel or spend money on company business.

## Approval

All business travel must be approved by your manager before booking. International travel also needs approval from the department head. Book through the company travel portal whenever possible.

## Flights

Economy class is the standard for all flights. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 8 hours and needs department head approval. Book flights at least 14 days in advance when you can.

## Accommodation

Hotels are reimbursed up to INR 9,000 per night in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata) and up to INR 6,000 per night elsewhere in India. For international trips the limit is USD 200 per night. Stays with friends or family can be claimed at a flat INR 1,500 per night.

## Meals and daily allowance

Employees travelling overnight receive a daily allowance of INR 2,000 in India or USD 60 abroad, which covers meals and local transport. Client meals are reimbursed separately with the itemised bill and the names of the attendees.

## Local transport

Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed for business trips. Use of a personal car is reimbursed at INR 12 per kilometre.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense, with a receipt for every item over INR 500. Claims are paid with the next monthly salary once your manager approves them. Claims submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Not reimbursed

Alcohol (except at approved client events), fines and penalties, personal entertainment, upgrades you chose yourself, and lost-luggage costs that the airline covers.
