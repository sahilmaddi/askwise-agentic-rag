# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

Northwind Labs is a fictional software company used for this demo. This handbook covers working hours, leave and remote work for all employees in India.

## Working hours

Our core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you can plan your day as you like, as long as you work 40 hours a week. Meetings should not be scheduled outside core hours without the attendees' agreement.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused days can be carried over to the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March. Leave requests of more than 5 consecutive days need approval from your manager at least 3 weeks in advance.

## Sick leave

Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. For absences longer than 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate is required. Sick leave cannot be carried over.

## Parental leave

Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Non-birthing parents receive 4 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption. Adoptive parents receive the same leave as birth parents.

## Public holidays

The company observes 10 public holidays per year. The list is published by HR every December for the following year.

## Remote work

Employees can work remotely up to 3 days per week. Teams agree on at least 2 shared office days. Fully remote arrangements need approval from the department head and HR. When working remotely you must use the company VPN and be reachable during core hours.

## Equipment

Every employee receives a laptop, which is replaced every 3 years. Remote workers can claim a one-time home office allowance of INR 25,000 for a chair, desk or monitor, with receipts.

## Notice period

The notice period is 30 days during probation and 60 days after confirmation. Probation lasts 6 months.
